One thing worth knowing before you view

Since July 2024, on a property priced up to eight million yen, the agent's commission can be a flat ¥330,000 — about 16.5% of the price on a ¥2,000,000 house. It is legal, and the agent has to explain it and get your agreement up front. Ask for the fee in writing before the viewing, not after you have fallen in love with the place.
